How it works

  1. Connect your wallet (MetaMask or Binance Wallet extension). Nothing is stored anywhere — there is no server.
  2. Hold an Agent ID. One claim per day, every day, to build your streak.
  3. Your first claim mints a soulbound badge. Your badge tokenId is your address as a number.
  4. Every claim is a transaction you confirm yourself in your own wallet.

No token. No rewards. No fees except gas. Every transaction is confirmed by you in your own wallet. AgentStreak never asks for your seed phrase, QR code, or pairing code.
